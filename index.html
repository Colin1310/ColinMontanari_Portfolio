02 / Background

About &
experience

Download my CV

My focus is interactive audio: building systems, integrating sounds and making them work in the engine. I trained in sound design and game audio in Montpellier and Montréal, then worked as a Technical Sound Designer at Gameloft Montréal.

JUL 2024 — APR 2025
Montréal, Canada

Gameloft Montréal

Technical Sound Designer

Worked on an open-world project with Unreal Engine 5 and Wwise: audio Blueprints, ambience systems, asset creation and integration, dynamic mixing, routing, profiling and debugging. Implemented attenuation, occlusion and obstruction systems.

FEB 2024 — JUN 2024
Campus ADN

Anima

Audio Integration Lead

Led audio integration for a 3D platformer built with Unreal Engine and Wwise. Responsibilities included team coordination, asset integration and audio systems.
